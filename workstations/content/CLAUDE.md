# Content — Workstation Rules

Inherits all rules from the root `CLAUDE.md`. Adds the content-specific rules below.

## Identity

This workstation handles writing, repurposing, and planning content — scripts, posts, newsletters, captions, and any copy that goes out under my name. It does **not** handle paid campaigns or analytics reporting (that's a separate concern), and it doesn't hold finished output (that lives in `projects/content/`).

## Workflow

1. Read the nearest `brand_context/voice-profile.md` and `positioning-icp.md` before drafting anything.

3. Draft.
